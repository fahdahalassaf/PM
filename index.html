The Company

Built on the conviction that execution is the rarest resource.

Pinnacle Maximal was founded on a straightforward observation: the gap between a strong idea and a successful product is almost never the idea. It's the people who can actually build it.

We operate at the intersection of strategic advisory and hands-on production. Our team has been in the rooms where critical decisions get made: market positioning, product architecture, supplier negotiations, early fundraising. We don't consult from a distance. We work alongside the people we advise.

Every engagement is structured, direct, and tied to real deliverables. We don't produce reports that live in shared drives. When we take on a project, we stay until the outcome is right. That might mean consulting, building, designing, or all three.

We also build and bring to market our own products, which means we operate under the same conditions we advise on. That keeps us honest.

Principles

How we approach every engagement

Clarity Before Activity

We define the problem precisely before we move. A well-framed problem is usually half solved.

No Orphaned Deliverables

Everything we produce connects to an outcome. If it doesn't move something forward, we don't make it.

Honest Counsel

If we think your plan has a problem, we say so. Our value is in accurate assessments, not comfortable ones.

Operational Skin in the Game

We run our own products through the same processes we advise on. We don't recommend things we wouldn't do ourselves.

Depth Over Breadth

We take on fewer engagements so each one gets real attention. We are not a volume practice.

Long-Term View

Decisions that look good in the short term but create structural problems later are not good decisions. We plan accordingly.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025  ·  Pinnacle Maximal

The short version

We collect only what we need, use it only for what we said, and don't pass it to anyone who doesn't need to see it. That's it. The rest of this just fills in the detail.

What we collect

When you reach out through our contact form, we collect your name, email address, company name, and whatever you write in the message. That information is used to respond to you and, if things move forward, to run the engagement.

We don't use ad networks, tracking pixels, or retargeting tools. We don't sell or rent your information. The only third parties who ever see it are the services we use to operate our business: email, cloud storage, invoicing. Nothing exotic.

What we do with it

  • Respond to your enquiry and set up any initial calls
  • Manage the working relationship if an engagement starts
  • Send invoices and project-related updates
  • Meet any legal or accounting obligations that apply to us

We don't send marketing emails. If you want us to stop contacting you, email us and we'll take care of it.

Storage and security

Client information sits in our business tools (email, shared drives) with access limited to the people on your project. We've taken standard steps to keep things secure. We won't make promises about absolute security because no system is airtight, but we take it seriously.

We don't hold payment card details. Invoicing goes through standard invoicing software and payments run through third-party processors.

How long we keep it

Active client records stay on file for the duration of the engagement and up to three years after for legal and accounting purposes. Enquiries that didn't turn into anything get deleted after six months. Want yours gone sooner? Email us and we'll sort it, unless there's a legal reason we can't.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email contact@pinnaclemaximal.com and we'll come back to you.

Cookies

No tracking cookies. The site may set a functional cookie to remember your theme preference. That's the extent of it. No analytics, no advertising platforms.

Updates

If this policy changes, the date at the top will reflect it. Our data practices are simple and not likely to shift, so don't expect surprises.

Questions

Email contact@pinnaclemaximal.com. We'll respond directly.

Terms of Service

Last updated: May 2025  ·  Pinnacle Maximal

Who these apply to

These terms cover all consultancy, design, manufacturing, R&D, and software work carried out by Pinnacle Maximal. Engaging our services, whether through a signed proposal, an email confirmation, or payment of an invoice, means you've accepted them. If a separate written agreement exists for your project, that document takes precedence where there's a conflict.

Scope

We agree on what's being done before we start doing it. Every engagement is confirmed in writing through a proposal, a statement of work, or an email acknowledgement. We don't start substantive work without a defined scope, and we don't expand scope without discussing and agreeing the change in writing first.

Payment

Hourly consultancy engagements are invoiced monthly. Invoices are due within 14 days. Project-based work is billed in stages agreed at the outset. Accounts outstanding past 30 days may result in work being paused.

We can charge interest on overdue amounts at 1.5% per month. We'd much rather have a direct conversation about it first, and that's always our first move.

Intellectual property

Work created specifically for your engagement transfers to you on full payment, unless the project agreement says otherwise. We keep the right to reference finished work in our portfolio unless you've asked for confidentiality. We'll honour that.

Tools, methods, and background IP we bring into an engagement stay ours. We use those across projects.

Confidentiality

Client information stays with us. We don't share project details, business specifics, or anything you've told us with outside parties unless you've said we can. We ask the same from you with respect to anything we share during the engagement.

Liability

We do the work properly and with genuine professional care. But consultancy work deals in judgement and advice, not certainty. Outcomes depend on far more than what we do. Our liability is capped at the fees paid for the specific engagement. We are not liable for lost profits, indirect losses, or consequential damages.

Ending an engagement

Either side can end an ongoing engagement with 14 days written notice. Work carried out up to that point is billed at the agreed rate. For fixed-scope projects, you're billed for completed milestones only, per the project agreement.

Disputes

We'd rather talk about it. If something goes wrong, a direct conversation is the starting point, not a formal process. If that doesn't resolve things, applicable law in the jurisdiction where Pinnacle Maximal is registered governs the dispute.

Updates

These terms may change from time to time. The version that applies to your engagement is the one in effect when it starts. Active clients will be notified of any significant changes.

Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2025  ·  Pinnacle Maximal

The short version

We don't do blanket refunds. We also don't hide behind policy when something has gone wrong on our end. If you have a real concern about the quality or value of work we delivered, bring it to us and we'll deal with it properly.

Free consultations

The introductory 30-minute call is free. There's nothing to refund and no obligation either way after the call ends.

Hourly work

Hours worked and invoiced are not refundable. The time and preparation have been spent. If a session wasn't useful or the work fell short of what you reasonably expected, tell us. We'll look at it honestly. If we agree there was a shortfall on our side, we'll fix it: additional work at no charge, a credit on the next invoice, or another fair remedy.

We don't refund hours because a business outcome didn't go the way you wanted. Consultancy is judgement and advice, not a performance guarantee. The difference between a delivery shortfall and a disappointing outcome is something we'll assess straight.

Project-based work

Completed milestones on fixed-scope projects (design, software, manufacturing) are not refunded. If a project ends early, you pay for what was completed to that point and nothing more, as set out in the Terms of Service. Advance payments for work not yet started are refunded in full.

If we're the problem

If we can't deliver on a confirmed engagement because of something on our end, we refund any prepayments in full and do what we reasonably can to help you find another path forward.

Raising a concern

Email contact@pinnaclemaximal.com. Tell us what happened. We'll come back to you within three business days. Talking it through is always the first step. Formal escalation is a last resort for both of us.
